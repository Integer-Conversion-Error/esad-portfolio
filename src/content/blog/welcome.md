Most of the work I do is invisible by design. Geofence validation. Payroll in
QuickBooks. A websocket for intercom audio that never leaves the LAN. You don't
see those things; you only notice them when they're missing. Writing publicly is
the opposite. Every word sits in the open and is easy to ignore, and that
asymmetry is exactly why it's worth doing.

I'm starting this notebook for the things I learn while building. The bias is
toward details that don't fit in a commit message. Why a layout ends up three
columns and not two. Why a SCSS variable stays at one value for three years.
Why code that's "clever" is usually the wrong call.
